# User Personas

> **Fill this in.** Define the people who use your product. Agents use personas to frame requirements, evaluate trade-offs, and prioritise accessibility and UX decisions. A feature that helps no defined persona is a feature worth questioning.

---

## How to Define a Persona

Copy this block and fill it in:

```markdown
## [Persona Name]

**Role / Title**: [e.g., "Small business owner", "DevOps engineer", "Data analyst"]
**Technical level**: Non-technical | Moderate | Technical | Expert
**Primary goal**: [The single most important thing they want to accomplish with your product]
**Pain points**:
- [Frustration 1]
- [Frustration 2]
**Usage context**: [When, where, and how often do they use your product?]
**Success looks like**: [What does a good outcome look like for this persona?]
**Quotes / insights**: [Optional — real or representative quotes that capture their mindset]
```
